What the numbers mean
Daily Prospecting Touches
Rolling two weeks, split into field visit, phone call and email. The visual that makes activity undeniable in a one on one.
JS Opportunity Aging
Your open pipeline in dollars, bucketed by age. Anything in an older bucket is a deal you have stopped working, not a deal that is still alive.
New Proposals Delivered Last 30 Days

Upcoming Scheduled BS Appts
Table of Account, Salesperson and Date. Your next two weeks. If this table is short, next month is already short.

How many surveys turned into a delivered proposal. A gap between this and BS to Close Won tells you which step you are losing on.
Prospecting Touches to BS Appointments
How much dialing and door knocking it takes you to book one survey.

First time here? Four things, once. Access, slicers, bookmarks, and who to ask.
  1. Check you can open one. Click Open report on your first tile. If it says access denied or the report does not exist, jump to step 4. Each app has to be shared with you by name.
  2. Set your slicers. Every report opens on somebody else's filters. Each report says which slicer to set under What is on it.
  3. Save a personal bookmark. With your slicers set, use Bookmarks in the report toolbar, add a personal bookmark, mark it default. After that the link lands on your view. Not confirmed on our licenses yet. If the Bookmarks menu is missing or will not save, set your slicers each visit and tell TJ.
  4. Access denied? TJ Roberts, tjroberts@gocitywide.com or 806-206-6192. TJ escalates to the app owner at the Support Center. Say which report and paste the link. It is almost always that the app has not been shared with your account yet.
